Frequently asked questions

Is Vidsay really free?

Yes. The free plan includes 25 videos and recordings up to 5 minutes each, with screen, camera and mic recording in the browser. No account and no install needed.

Where are my recordings stored?

On your own device. Vidsay records in your browser and downloads the video file (.webm) to your computer. There is no cloud storage in this version, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Vidsay runs in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) using built-in recording. On desktop you can share your screen; on your phone it records with your camera. Click Record and pick what to share.

How is Vidsay different from Loom?

Loom stores your videos in the cloud and charges for longer recordings and AI features. Vidsay is a lightweight, browser-only alternative: your recordings stay on your device, the recorder is free, and there is no account to create.

What is the 5-minute limit?

Free recordings stop automatically at 5 minutes. Pro ($12/user/month) removes the cap and the watermark, and adds longer recordings — AI summaries and transcripts are marked as coming soon and are not available yet.
