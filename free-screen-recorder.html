How recording in a browser works

Modern desktop browsers have screen capture and video recording built in. On desktop, when you press record, Vidsay asks the browser to share your screen, combines it with your camera on a canvas, and records the result with the MediaRecorder API. On your phone it records your camera instead — phones can't share their screen. The video is assembled on your device — it never travels to a server, because there is no server in this version. When you stop, the file downloads as a .webm video you can send anywhere.

That also sets the limits honestly: screen sharing needs Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a desktop computer, and Safari's support is limited. If your browser can't do it, Vidsay tells you so instead of failing silently.
